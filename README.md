# 🌊 BlueGuard — Save Our Seas, One Action at a Time

**BlueGuard** is a marine conservation website designed to raise awareness about ocean pollution and inspire people to protect marine life. It encourages individuals, students, and communities to take action through beach cleanups, plastic reduction challenges, and other environmental activities.

Our mission is to turn small actions into a big wave of change for our oceans. 🐢

## 🌍 The Problem

Marine life faces many threats, including:
- Plastic pollution and litter in oceans and on beaches.
- Marine animals becoming entangled in discarded waste.
- Damage to coral reefs and other marine habitats.
- Limited awareness of how everyday human activities affect the ocean.

## 💡 Our Solution

BlueGuard makes marine conservation engaging, educational, and actionable. Our platform helps people learn about marine life, participate in environmental challenges, and track their contributions to protecting the ocean.

### ✨ Key Features

- **🏠 Interactive Homepage:** Introduces BlueGuard and encourages visitors to take action.
- **🐬 Discover Marine Life:** Learn about sea turtles, dolphins, whales, coral reefs, and other marine species.
- **♻️ Ocean Rescue Missions:** Explore challenges such as collecting litter, reducing single-use plastics, and organising cleanups.
- **📊 Impact Dashboard:** Display environmental contributions, including waste collected, cleanup activities, and volunteers involved.
- **🗺️ Pollution Hotspot Map:** Help users identify reported litter hotspots and cleanup locations.
- **🏆 Ocean Heroes:** Motivate users with points, badges, and a leaderboard.
- **📸 Cleanup Reports:** Allow participants to record cleanup activities and submit evidence of their work.

## 🎯 Our Goals

1. Raise awareness about marine pollution and its effects on ocean ecosystems.
2. Encourage responsible waste disposal and recycling.
3. Motivate young people to participate in environmental conservation.
4. Support community-led cleanup activities.
5. Measure and celebrate progress toward cleaner oceans.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** — Structures the website.
- **CSS3** — Provides styling, layouts, animations, and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** — Powers interactive features and user activities.
- **LocalStorage** — Can save progress and scores locally in the browser.
- **Leaflet.js** — Can be used to create an interactive pollution hotspot map.
